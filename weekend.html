Calendar of Events

Golf on Thursday, pickleball Saturday morning, and the main event Saturday night. Come for one of them or all three.

Thursday · Sept 17

Class golf outing

The Heritage Golf Links, Tucker

Tee off our five decades and catch up with your classmates Thursday morning. Mark Kaufman is organizing and can give you details.

Who to contact
Mark Kaufman 404-550-2402 mfkauf@yahoo.com

Saturday · Sept 19

Pickleball

Morning

Atlanta Pickleball Center

Pickleball was not a sport when we were in high school, but I suspect a fair number of us are now playing. If you’d like to play in a round robin with some of your classmates, connect with Victor or Kent for details.

Who to contact
Victor Haley vhaley1600@aol.com
Kent Alexander kent.alexander@me.com

Saturday · Sept 19

The Main Event

6:30 PM to 10:30 PM

The Ansley Golf Club

An evening of memories, reconnection and fun. We kick off at 6:30 pm and end on the earlier of 10:30 pm or your bedtime!

Hors d’oeuvres, dinner, dessert, beer and wine — all included.

Complimentary valet parking.

Suggested donation

$100 per person

We expect the total cost to exceed ticket revenue, so if you are so inclined, we would appreciate any additional contribution you want to make.

Guests are welcome. Bring a spouse, a partner, or the classmate who needs a ride.

Coming?

Please send in your reservation right away so we can meet venue deadlines — fifty years is worth a big celebration and we hope you all can make it!
