A note from the committee

Fifty years is a strange number. Long enough that the school itself is gone, short enough that most of us can still picture the halls on a Friday afternoon in September.

There are classmates we still haven’t reached. If you know where someone is, or you have a phone number that still works, send it to us. That’s the one part of this we can’t do alone.

Come sit with the people who were there.
